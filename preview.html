    Before you decide

    Questions buyers ask us first.

    Why buy a detached factory instead of a semi-D or terrace?

    You have no shared walls and no neighbour's trucks blocking your gate. The setback all around the building leaves space for trucks to turn, load and wait. Every unit sits on at least 0.99 acre.

    What's the difference between Type A, B and C?

    Type A (4 units) is the intermediate lot at 0.99 acre with 22,833 sq ft built-up. Type B (8 units) is intermediate on 1.20–1.24 acres with 25,865 sq ft built-up. Type C (9 units) is a corner lot on 1.19–1.25 acres with 25,844 sq ft built-up. All three have production space, offices, a canteen, washrooms and a surau.

    How accessible is the site for my trucks and workers?

    You can reach it today through Sungai Buaya Interchange on the North–South Expressway (E1). A second connection to Bukit Beruntung Interchange is planned through the industrial park, with a target of Q1 2028. ECRL Bandar Serendah station is about 8 km away.

    What happens at a private viewing?

    We take you round the site, go through the floor plans for the lots you shortlisted, and show you the latest availability and pricing. There's no obligation to buy.
